Reasoning

Portfolio thesis: Favor high-quality businesses with either strong, corroborated operating momentum or defensive/low-volatility technical support, while limiting exposure where fundamentals and market structure conflict. The portfolio balances AAPL, NVDA and AMZN growth with KO, JNJ and PG defensiveness, and retains selective cyclical/energy exposure through XOM and CAT; NEE is kept modest because technical and policy support conflict with weak cash-flow and leverage evidence. Regime view: The reports indicate a mixed, risk-sensitive regime: several assets have supportive medium-term trends, but volatility, drawdowns, trade/export-control risks and disagreement between fundamentals and price action are significant. Position sizing therefore emphasizes diversification and keeps a modest cash reserve rather than concentrating in the strongest individual stances.
